# ⚡ Odin Hates Litter

### Clean up. Face Odin’s wrath. Earn your reward.

**Valheim events by Dova.**

Littering builds Odin’s wrath and can trigger cleanup challenges or enemy attacks. Build a Litter Bin, discover biome altars or take an offering out to sea.

## ⚔️ Trials, raids & ocean battles

- **Cleanup challenges and combat events** with enemy waves, mini-bosses, boss phases and reinforcements.
- **Buildable Litter Bins and biome altars** for starting events.
- **Ocean battles** with craftable Sea Offerings and floating loot. Altar docks sink after a warning during combat, then rise when the event ends.
- **Six difficulty presets**, from **Relaxed** to **Cataclysm**, with shark and serpent choices for Ocean events.
- **Ready votes and Endless checkpoints** to start the next wave early or cash out earned rewards.
- **Boss guard phases**, short shields and elemental attacks, with separate bin controls.
- **Event music, weather and arena boundaries**, plus limited returns after death.
- **Clear enemy markers** with Litter Bin icons and an extra boss icon for bosses and mini-bosses.

New land altars check their full starting arena for dry ground, lava and uneven terrain. Existing altars stay in place.

## 🎁 Rewards worth fighting for

- **Personal completion rewards**, with guaranteed combat equipment rolls in presets.
- **Stamina, health and Eitr blessings** lasting **10–25 minutes**.
- **Personal contribution targets**, including credit for blocking event attacks.
- **Reward cooldown warnings**, optional equipment bad-luck protection and saved reward history.
- **Choose one of two equipment rewards** after Nightmare or Cataclysm Boss and Endless victories.
- **Stronger blessings stay active** after easier events. Equipment repeat history carries between modes.
- **Heavy, cursed and bonus litter**, with limits on extra enemies and coins. Late helpers stop adding litter near the end.

## 🗺️ Keep track of your trials

Your **altar journal** records discovered altars, difficulty, readiness, cooldowns and your last result.

Separate HUD controls cover each mode and altar type. Notices stay clear of the tracker, and players receive their own results.

Event progress and starting settings are saved. Cooldowns begin when events end, and replacing a player bin does **not** reset them.

## 🛠️ Server & admin controls

- Adjust each event type, altar and bin with **Apply, Discard and Undo**.
- Inspect active events, locate stranded enemies with admin map markers and return them to the arena.
- Automatic recovery helps events finish when enemies become stranded.
- Small spawn batches and a shared server limit control the number of living event enemies.
- **Reset all configs** restores the 1.6.0 defaults and Balanced mode settings, with confirmation and Undo. Saved bins, presets and cooldowns are kept.

## 🚀 Get started

**Start a bin event**

Use a Litter Bin with **Alt + E**, then press again to confirm.

**Take an offering to sea**

Craft a **Sea Offering** and use the same two-step confirmation while at sea.

**Open your altar journal**

Go to **F1 → General and display**, or enter `odin_journal` in the console.

**Manage active events as an admin**

Go to **F1 → Admin and server → Event management**.

## 🧩 Mod integrations

**Epic Loot is required. The other integrations are optional.**

Supports **Creature Level and Loot Control**, **Therzie’s Monstrum**, **World Advancement Progression**, **Wacky Epic MMO System** and **Resurrection**.

Also supports **Venture Floating Items**, **Venture Location Reset**, **Upgrade World**, **Configuration Manager**, **Variant Announcements**, **ServerGuard** and **Discord Connector**.

## 📦 Install & requirements

- **BepInExPack Valheim 5.4.2351+**
- **Jotunn 2.30.1+**
- **Epic Loot 0.14.10+**

Install through your mod manager, or copy the ZIP’s `plugins` folder into `BepInEx`. Keep one copy of `OdinHatesLitter.dll` and restart the game or server.

**Install the same Odin Hates Litter build on the server and every client. Keep your config files when updating.**
